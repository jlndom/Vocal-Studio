What this assessment can and cannot tell you

  • Pitch, timing and phrase-level observations are strongest when the voice is clear and the reference is suitable.
  • Diction is assessed against supplied lyrics and the audible performance; transcription can mishear sung words.
  • Emotion, tone and technique are perceptual coaching judgements, not medical diagnoses or laboratory measurements.
  • The app reports uncertainty rather than inventing a precise score when the audio does not support one.
